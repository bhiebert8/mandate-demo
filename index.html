Compliance monitoring for ETF issuers

Mandate checks each fund's holdings every day against the limits in its prospectus, its SAI and the 1940 Act. It is for small and mid-size U.S. ETF issuers and is installed on your own Windows machines.

Request a demo How it works

What it does

  • Daily checks

    Rules run on a schedule, or as soon as the day's holdings files arrive. Market holidays are skipped.

  • Exceptions

    Each breach or warning gets an owner, notes, an acknowledgement and an active or passive classification.

  • Your whole team

    Everyone signs in with their own account. Notes, sign-offs and changes carry their name. No per-seat fees.

  • Bloomberg fields

    Rules can test issuer, ratings, industry, country, duration and other Bloomberg fields, looked up by ISIN or CUSIP.

  • Audit trail

    Changes are recorded, and each rule keeps its version history.

  • Reports and alerts

    Excel reports for each run. Alerts by email, Slack or Microsoft Teams.

Results by fund

Each fund page lists its rules with the current value, the limit and the remaining headroom.

Setup and data

Windows
Mandate is installed on Windows machines that you control.
Bloomberg
Your own Bloomberg connection: Terminal Desktop API, or Server API/B-PIPE. A Bloomberg license is not included.
Holdings files
The daily holdings files your fund administrator or custodian already sends. Issuer, ratings, industry and country come from Bloomberg, so the files do not need to carry them.
Implementation
A one-time service: we install the software, connect Bloomberg, map your files and build your rules from your prospectus and SAI.
Updates
Updates install in one step. Mandate makes a verified backup of your data before every upgrade, and your history and rules carry over.
Your data
Holdings data stays on your machines. Mandate sends us one daily license check-in with license and version details and active fund and user counts. If you turn on alerts, they go out by email through your own mail server, or to Slack or Microsoft Teams. See Security and deployment.
