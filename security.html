Security and deployment

Mandate is installed on your own Windows machines. Holdings data is stored and processed there.

Data flow

Into Mandate

Holdings filesFrom your administrator or custodian, by folder, email inbox or upload

Bloomberg reference dataThrough your own Terminal or B-PIPE connection

Your Windows machine

Mandate
  • Rules and results
  • Exceptions, audit trail and Excel reports
  • Local database, protected by the machine's disk encryption

Out of your network

Daily license check-inTo us: license and version details, active fund and user counts

Alerts, if turned onEmail through your mail server, or to Slack or Microsoft Teams

Details

Deployment
Installed on Windows machines that you control. Holdings data stays on those machines.
Sent to us
A daily license check-in with license and version details and active fund and user counts. It contains no holdings data.
Alerts
Alert emails, sent through your own mail server, and Slack and Microsoft Teams messages go out only if you turn alerts on. The mail server password is kept in Windows Credential Manager.
Bloomberg
Requires your own Bloomberg: Terminal Desktop API, or Server API/B-PIPE. Securities are looked up by ISIN or CUSIP through that connection. If Bloomberg returns no data, the run stops instead of storing partial results.
Data at rest
The local database relies on the machine's disk encryption, such as BitLocker. Turn on disk encryption on every machine that runs Mandate.
Implementation
A one-time service: installation, Bloomberg connection, mapping your administrator's files, and building your rules from your prospectus and SAI.
Current status
Version 0.1, in private testing. The installer is not code-signed yet. Mandate has no SOC 2 report.
