Try the app in your browser

A static copy of Mandate with fictional data. Nothing is installed and nothing you do leaves your browser tab.

Start fresh The app as a new client sees it on first launch. Walk through setup: Bloomberg in demo mode, a data feed from the bundled sample files, funds, rules from templates, and the first run. Harborview Capital A fully set-up workspace: five fictional ETFs, 21 rules and nine weeks of history, with open exceptions to review and a run to sign off.

Desktop browser recommended. Mandate is a Windows desktop app; this demo has no engine behind it, so a few things (license keys, downloads, mail servers) are switched off.
