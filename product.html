How Mandate works

Each day Mandate reads your holdings file, looks up reference data in Bloomberg, runs your rules and records the results.

Holdings files

Mandate reads the daily holdings files your fund administrator or custodian already sends. Files can arrive in a watched folder, in an email inbox or by upload.

Setting up a feed includes a column-mapping step for the file's existing layout.

Email inbox ingestion is included in the Professional and Enterprise plans.

Reference data from Bloomberg

Issuer, ratings, industry and country are looked up in Bloomberg by ISIN or CUSIP, so your administrator's files do not need to carry them.

The rule builder has a searchable catalog of Bloomberg fields, such as ratings, durations, classifications and country of risk.

Mandate uses your own Bloomberg connection: Terminal Desktop API, or Server API/B-PIPE. If Bloomberg returns no data, the run stops instead of storing partial results.

Rules

During implementation we build your rules from your prospectus and SAI. Mandate ships with 25 rule templates, and implementation draws on our library of 53. Each rule keeps its version history.

Rule types

  • Exposure limits and ranges, including 80% names-rule tests
  • Issuer concentration
  • 1940 Act 75/5/10 diversification
  • RIC diversification
  • Industry concentration
  • Weighted averages: duration, WAM, WAL
  • Per-holding limits
  • Prohibited and restricted lists
  • OFAC sanctions screening
  • Fund-level metrics, such as a Cayman subsidiary cap
  • Rule 2a-7 money market fund tests
  • Total-assets gross-up for reverse repo
  • Rule 22e-4 illiquid and highly liquid limits
  • Rule 18f-4 VaR limits, with the reference index's VaR checked from Bloomberg

Exceptions

Each breach or warning opens an exception. You can assign an owner, add notes, acknowledge it and classify it as an active or passive breach.

Team, records and alerts

  • Team accounts

    Each person signs in with their own account and role: admin, reviewer or viewer. Unlimited users on every plan.

  • Shared workspace

    One office computer or server runs the checks. Colleagues join by invite from their own machines and see the same results.

  • Audit trail

    Records each change with the user and time. Each rule keeps its version history.

  • Excel reports

    Each run's results can be downloaded as an Excel workbook.

  • Alerts

    Alerts by email through your own mail server, or to Slack or Microsoft Teams. Off until you turn them on.

  • Safe updates

    Before each upgrade, Mandate makes a verified backup of your data. Your history and rules carry over.

Enterprise plan: intraday and pre-trade checks from an OMS connection. In pilot.
